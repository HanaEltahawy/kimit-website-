Mirasol by RAK Properties

📍 Raha Island, Mina Al Arab, Ras Al Khaimah | Starting AED 1,618,000

Project General Facts

Mirasol is a resort-inspired residential development located on Mina Al Arab, offering a refined island lifestyle surrounded by panoramic sea views and natural beauty. The project features a selection of studios, 1 and 2-bedroom apartments, spacious 3-bedroom duplexes, and exclusive penthouses, all designed to capture breathtaking sunsets and uninterrupted waterfront vistas from cascading rooftops.

The architecture of Mirasol reflects modern elegance through clean lines, sleek façades, and thoughtfully crafted structures that harmonize with the surrounding environment. Each residence is designed to enhance natural light, ventilation, and views, creating a seamless connection between indoor spaces and the outdoors. From lush greenery to sparkling water features and distant mountains, every element contributes to a serene and visually striking living experience.

The interiors feature elegant designs with seamless indoor-outdoor flow, connecting living areas to expansive terraces. Kitchens are equipped to ensure everyday convenience, while the residences are offered unfurnished, allowing homeowners the freedom to personalize their living space according to their own style and preferences.

Location Description & Benefits

Raha Island, nestled in the Emirate of Ras Al-Khaimah, UAE, is an enchanting destination known for its natural beauty and tranquil atmosphere. This idyllic island offers a perfect escape from the hustle and bustle of city life, attracting visitors with its pristine beaches, crystal-clear waters, and lush landscapes.

Raha Island boasts stunning panoramic views of the Arabian Gulf, with its azure waters lapping gently against sandy shores. The island is surrounded by verdant mangroves and dotted with palm trees, creating a picturesque setting that is perfect for relaxation and rejuvenation.

Visitors to Raha Island can indulge in a variety of activities, from sunbathing and swimming to snorkeling and kayaking. The island's rich marine life and coral reefs make it a popular spot for underwater exploration. For those who prefer land-based activities, there are numerous walking and cycling trails that allow you to explore the island's natural beauty at your own pace.
